To Re-cap I'm looking at one potential approach to answering one of managements favourite questions for security people "Are we secure yet"
The idea is to break that question down into categories of threats and the controls in place to defend against them.
The 2nd category of threats to look at is
Internal Targeted Threats
Depending on who you listen to this category may constitute the vast majority of attack on a companies information and includes things like Internal fraud and system abuse by disgruntled employees.
Obviously the controls that are useful for mitigating Non-Targeted threats are actually going to be less than useful against Internal attacks. Anti-Virus won't block someone with legitimate access to a system from copying all the information onto a usb stick and walking out the door...

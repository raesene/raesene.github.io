I've been reading the series of Posts between Richard Bejtlich and Alex Hutton and whilst I don't want to render any comments on their discussion directly (I know a minefield when I see one) it did get me thinking about Security "truisms" and risk assessments.
There are some things in security which seem to be accepted wisdom and not really subject to a lot of debate/risk analysis and one of them seems, to me, to be password policies. On that note I've started reading Authentication: From passwords to public keys. It's been a very interesting read so far and helps to actually provide some framework to making decisions on sensible authentication strategies
